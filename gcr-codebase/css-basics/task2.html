Jaipur, The Pink City

Overview Food Culture Travel Tips

Overview

Jaipur is the capital of Rajasthan, famous for its rose-pink buildings, grand forts, and rich royal history. It forms part of India's Golden Triangle.

Local Food

Don't miss the dal baati churma, pyaaz kachori, and the famous Rajasthani thali, served in traditional havelis around the old city.

Culture & Festivals

Jaipur comes alive during Teej and the Jaipur Literature Festival, with folk music, puppet shows, and vibrant markets selling handicrafts and textiles.

Travel Tips

Visit between October and March for pleasant weather, and set aside at least three days to properly explore Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, and the City Palace.
