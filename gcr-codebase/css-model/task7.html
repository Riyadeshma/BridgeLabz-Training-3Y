Typography & Text Styling

The Art of Readable Web Typography

Good typography is invisible when done well, yet it quietly shapes how comfortably a reader moves through a page.

Typography relies on a handful of core properties working together. The font-family determines the overall character of the text, while font-weight and font-size control emphasis and hierarchy. Careful use of line-height keeps paragraphs from feeling cramped, and letter-spacing can open up tightly-set headings for better legibility.
